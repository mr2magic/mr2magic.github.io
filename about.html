About

Four decades in.
Still building.

Dan Horn has spent four decades solving problems in technology — and he's very particular about which ones are worth solving.

His résumé spans Turner Broadcasting and CNN's digital infrastructure, an international disaster recovery initiative at DHL Globalmail, and field deployments across the British Virgin Islands, the UK, and Japan. He holds a US patent for his work on Narrow Band Traffic Control Units (US 20200059826). Along the way he's navigated payment gateways, PCI and HIPAA compliance, enterprise cloud migrations, and the full stack of problems that emerge when systems absolutely cannot go down.

These days that same instinct — find the thing that should exist and isn't done right yet — drives everything at Snuffy Rhino Productions. No big team, no growth hacks, no notifications begging for your attention. Just things made carefully, by hand, on purpose.

The Apps

Four problems.
Four solutions.

Manuscan

iOS · Manuscript Analysis

Import any EPUB and Manuscan uses Claude AI to dissect your story at every level — acts, chapters, scenes, beats — then scores it across 15 continuity dimensions and hands you a full report. Health score 0–100. Export to Markdown, HTML, or Fountain.

Silent screen recording — no audio or narration.

See the app

Ragion

iOS · RAG Document Q&A

Build private knowledge bases from PDFs, EPUBs, spreadsheets, code files, and 16 other formats. Ask questions in plain language using Claude, ChatGPT, or a fully local Ollama model. Every answer cites the exact passage it came from. A sophisticated iOS implementation built on the open-source RAGFlow project.

See the app

Ingredient Getter

iOS · Recipe to Shopping List

Snap up to 10 recipe photos. Claude AI extracts every ingredient. A 700+ item lookup table organizes the whole list by aisle, matched to your store's layout. Merge multiple recipes, check off as you shop. All data stays on device.

See the app

Writers Wellspring

Web App · Story Analysis

Runs your manuscript through Claude AI to extract the complete narrative structure — acts down to individual beats — then surfaces continuity issues across character consistency, pacing, theme, and more. Export the full analysis as Markdown.

See the app
The Writing

Fiction, nonfiction,
and one AI experiment.

The books started the same way as the apps — with something that didn't exist yet and should.

Adventures of Tumbleton T.

Mystery Series · Tweens

A Sherlock Holmes-style mystery series where every character is an endangered species. Tumbleton T. is a dapper white rhino in a three-piece suit. Pete is his razor-sharp French Bulldog partner. Together they take on cases that are always bigger than they first appear. The Case of the Missing Grizzly is Book One.

See the book

Dinosaur

Short Fiction

A short story told from the inside — literally. The world as seen through the eyes of a dinosaur: ancient, enormous, and quietly attentive to a landscape built for creatures far smaller and faster. A meditation on scale and what perspective actually means.

Buy on Amazon

Dark Times In Limbo

Dark Fiction · Work in Progress

Stephen King tension, Gilligan protagonist. A lovably hapless character gets stuck in a situation that keeps getting stranger and more inescapable — the kind of story where the dread is real but so is the dark humor, and you root for the hero precisely because everything is stacked against them.

Technologists Never Retire, They Just Reboot

Nonfiction · AI-Generated

What does a lifelong technologist do when the career ends but the curiosity doesn't? This one started as an experiment — the entire book was written by AI — and turned into something genuinely useful: a map of the tools, communities, and disciplines that keep a retired technologist sharp, relevant, and off the bleeding edge of the budget. The meta-irony of an AI writing a book about staying current with AI was not lost on anyone.
