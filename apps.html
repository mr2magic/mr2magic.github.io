iOS Apps

Built to do one thing well.

Four apps, four problems, no bloat.

Manuscan

Your manuscript, analyzed.

Import any EPUB and Manuscan uses Claude AI to dissect your story at every level — from act structure down to individual beats — then scores it across 15 continuity dimensions and delivers a full report.

  • Structure extraction — Acts → Chapters → Scenes → Beats
  • 15-dimension continuity analysis
  • Health score 0–100
  • Character & location maps
  • EPUB import
  • Export to Markdown, HTML, or Fountain

Silent screen recording — no audio or narration.

Ragion

Chat with your documents.

Build private knowledge bases from PDFs, EPUBs, spreadsheets, code files, and 16 other formats. Then ask questions in plain language — using Claude, ChatGPT, or a fully local Ollama model — and get answers that cite the exact passage they came from.

  • 20+ file types
  • Hybrid BM25 + vector search
  • Claude, ChatGPT, or Ollama
  • Source citations on every answer
  • Agent workflows — RAG Q&A, Summarizer, Multi-Hop Researcher
  • Fully on-device with Ollama
Ingredient Getter

Photo a recipe. Walk in ready.

Snap up to 10 recipe photos and Ingredient Getter uses Claude AI to pull out every ingredient. A 700+ item lookup table then organizes the whole list by aisle — matched to your store's layout.

  • Recipe photo scanning (up to 10 photos)
  • Claude AI ingredient extraction
  • 700+ item aisle lookup
  • Customizable store layout
  • Merge multiple recipes into one list
  • AI recipe suggestion chat
  • All data stays on device
Writers Wellspring

Story analysis, no editor required.

Writers Wellspring reads your manuscript with Claude AI and extracts the complete narrative structure — acts, chapters, scenes, beats — then surfaces continuity problems across character consistency, pacing, theme, and more. Export the full analysis as Markdown.

  • Narrative structure extraction
  • Continuity analysis
  • Health score
  • Character, location & motif maps
  • Narrative overview, themes & style
  • Markdown export
