The Case of the Missing Grizzly

by Dan Horn

Adventures of Tumbleton T. · Book 1

A Sherlock Holmes-style mystery series for tweens — where every character is an endangered species. Tumbleton T., a dapper white rhino in a three-piece suit, and Pete, his razor-sharp French Bulldog partner, have a missing Grizzly to find and not many leads to follow.

Dinosaur

by Dan Horn

A short story told from the inside — the world as seen through ancient eyes, moving through a landscape built for creatures far smaller and faster. A quiet meditation on scale, perspective, and what you notice when you're the biggest thing in the room.

Dark Times In Limbo

by Dan Horn

Told with the slow-building dread of Stephen King, but starring a protagonist with all the luck of Gilligan. A lovably hapless character navigates a situation that keeps getting stranger and more inescapable — the kind of story where you root for the hero precisely because everything is stacked against them.

by Dan Horn

What does a lifelong technologist do when the career ends but the curiosity doesn't? Written entirely by AI, this book maps the tools, communities, and disciplines that keep a retired technologist sharp, relevant, and off the bleeding edge of the budget.
